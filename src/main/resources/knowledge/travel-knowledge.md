# Tokyo
Tokyo itinerary usually works best when grouped by region: Asakusa and Ueno together, Shibuya and Harajuku together, and Ginza with nearby waterfront areas on another day.
Rainy days in Tokyo are usually better for museums, observation decks, shopping streets, and indoor food markets.
Popular first-time picks include Senso-ji, Tokyo Skytree, Shibuya Scramble, Meiji Shrine, Ueno Park, Tsukiji Outer Market, and teamLab style digital museums.

# Osaka
Osaka trips usually balance food, nightlife, and nearby day trips. Dotonbori, Shinsaibashi, Osaka Castle, Umeda Sky Building, and Shinsekai are common anchors.
If the trip includes Kyoto or Nara, those should usually be treated as separate day plans instead of being mixed into a dense Osaka city day.

# Kyoto
Kyoto routes are best organized by area and temple opening hours. Higashiyama can fill a full day, Arashiyama usually needs a half day to full day, and Fushimi Inari works well early morning or evening.
Peak seasons require lighter schedules because transit and queues increase sharply.

# Travel heuristics
Avoid scheduling too many cross-city hops on the same day.
Outdoor-heavy days should be cross-checked against weather.
For family trips, keep no more than two hard anchors per day.
For budget-focused trips, cluster attractions in walkable zones and prefer transit passes.
