# Guizhou
Chinese destination aliases: 贵州, Guizhou, Guizhou

## Overview
Guizhou (贵州; Gùizhōu) is a province in southwestern China.

## Cities
1 Anshun — tourist center and home of the Huangguoshu Waterfalls
2 Guiyang — the capital
3 Kaili — gateway to the Miao and Dong minority regions
 Libo County
4 Liupanshui — called the Cool City, for its low average summer temperature
5 Zunyi — communist pilgrimage site, and also administratively covers the town of Maotai, which produces China's national liquor
6 Xingyi — county level city in southwest China with rich karst topography such as Maling Valley, Ten Thousand Peaks Forest, etc.
7 Weining — home of Cao Hai Lake, known for its 170+ bird species including the black-necked crane
8 Taijiang — has the title of "the first county of Miao nationality in the world"
9 Tongren — gateway to Mount Fanjing (Fanjingshan)
10 Zhenyuan — historic city in the county of the same name, surrounded by karst scenery

## Other destinations
Site of Hailongtun Tusi Fortress, a castle ruin which is one of three Tusi sites on the UNESCO World Heritage List
Fanjingshan, a part of the Wuling mountain range which due to its unique geology and diverse wildlife was inscribed on the world heritage list in 2018
Miao and Dong villages: 11 Lang De, a Miao village 20 km south of Kaili; Xijiang (西江), large traditional Miao village located in LeiGongshan national park; and 12 Zhaoxing (肇兴), idyllic Dong minority town nestling in a valley
Xiaoqikong and Daqikong, two marvelously lush scenic areas in Libo County
Huangguoshu Falls: one of the famous waterfalls in China, located in Anshun City, 77.8 meters high and 101 meters wide, it is known as "the first waterfall in China".
Libo small seven holes: located in Libo County, is a magnificent Karst landscape scenic spot, with seven natural bridge caves, as well as streams, waterfalls and landscape beauty.

Guiyang Qingyan ancient town: this is a well-preserved Ming and Qing ancient buildings, with a long history and unique cultural customs.
Zhaoxing Dong Zhai: Zhaoxing Village, located in southeast Guizhou, is one of the representative places of Dong culture. Tourists can enjoy the architecture, clothing, song and dance and customs of Dong nationality.
Qianhu Miao Village in southeastern Guizhou: this is a large Miao settlement, famous for its spectacular wooden buildings, rich Miao culture and beautiful natural environment.
Guiyang Huaxi Park: it is a scenic spot in Guiyang City, with beautiful lakes, mountains and gardens, suitable for leisure walk and enjoy the natural beauty.
p Tongren Wanfeng Forest: this place is famous for its strange Karst landform, with countless stone peaks, caverns and streams, which is very suitable for adventure and photography enthusiasts.
Zhenyuan, an ancient town in southern Guizhou, preserves the architectural style of the Ming and Qing dynasties, such as the ancient city walls, hanging feet and temples, and is a cultural resort with a long history.

## Understand
Guizhou, located in the southwest of China, is a mountainous and multi-ethnic province.

## Get around
Local roads in Guiyang city are in good condition and taxis are plentiful. However, to travel from one city to another and then further into the countryside, roads can be in very poor condition, or only under construction (travelling together with bulldozers...).
Taxis in smaller cities tend to gather several travellers into one car in order to compensate for the very low fares (¥3 as a start fare for the first 2.5 km in 2013). In some smaller cities, the meter is hardly ever used and there are "fixed" fares from the station to the city, from the city to some center of interests.... So, be prepared to be flexible in that respect.
Trains are quite reliable, although not as comfortable as the newest high speed trains in richer provinces.

## See
The area is mountainous, and there are many opportunities for hiking. The paths can be slippery due to animal excrement and the bushes can be thorny. There is much beautiful scenery including valley views and distant horizons. One of the greatest attractions to Guizhou is the Huangguoshu Waterfall, known to be the biggest waterfall in China. The eastern area of Guizhou is very green and you can see many rice fields in terrace as well as go through numerous small charming villages where you can also rest in guest houses. The China Danxia landscape near Chisui are inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage List.

Mount Fanjing is the highest peak of the Wuling Mountains in the Fanjingshan National Nature Reserve. Fanjingshan is a sacred mountain in Chinese Buddhism, considered to be the bodhimaṇḍa of the Maitreya Buddha, and listed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2018.

## Do
There are many walks and places to visit in the vicinity of the capital Guiyang.
Guizhou is home to many ethnic minorities (mainly the Miao and the Dong). Festivals are held all year round, and are great ways to experience the local customs.

## Eat
There is a wide variety of foods available, with the local taste being for spicy dishes. The food can be very tasty. The hot pot can be excellent.
Another specialty is the sour spicy fish soup that can be tasted in many cities in Guizhou. Barbecue on the streets is a local favorite as are as all sorts of rice noodles and pasta, mixed with ingredients such as pickled vegetables.

## Drink
Moutai and home made rice wine made by villagers.

## Stay safe
While hiking in the mountains, watch out for the narrow ledges. Also, always remember sunscreen and water while enjoying the countryside and watching wildlife.
