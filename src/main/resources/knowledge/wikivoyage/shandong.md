# Shandong
Chinese destination aliases: 山东, Shandong, Shandong

## Overview
Shandong (山东 Shāndōng) is a province in China. Also referred to as Chiloo (齐鲁 Qilu), The Giant State (Shandong people are known to be the tallest in the country), and the hometown of Confucius. 
The name means east of the mountains. Shandong is a coastal province situated by the Bohai Sea to the north and the Yellow Sea to the south. The Yellow River runs through the province.
Main attractions are Qufu (the birthplace of Confucius), Mount Tai (holy mountain in Chinese belief, the most climbed mountain in China), Qingdao (nice port city with beaches and German buildings) and Yantai (holiday city with lots of European buildings). The capital, Jinan (a city of springs where water and willows are famous), is renowned for its artesian springs and serves as a transport hub for the region.

## See
Mount Tai: A World Heritage Site, enjoy sunrise views from the summit and experience the majestic scenery of the "Most Dominant of the Five Mountains."
Confucius Temple, Cemetery, and Mansion in Qufu: The hometown of Confucius, learn about Confucian culture and history.

## Do
Climb Mount Tai: Choose to hike or take the cable car for a pilgrimage-like experience.
Qingdao Beach Activities: In summer, enjoy swimming, beach volleyball, and strolling along the coast.

## Eat
Shandong pancakes: a local breakfast specialty, thin, crispy and delicious.
Shandong cuisine classics: sweet and sour carp, nine-turn large intestine, Dezhou braised chicken.

## Drink
Tsingtao Beer: Famous both at home and abroad, best enjoyed chilled in summer.
Spring water tea: Jinan spring water can be used directly to make tea, which has a refreshing taste.

## Stay safe
When climbing Mount Tai or other scenic areas, pay attention to safety railings and wear comfortable hiking shoes.
Be mindful of personal belongings, especially at popular scenic spots and beaches.

## Go next

Henan Province: Such as Luoyang and Shaolin Temple, explore the history and culture of the Central Plains.
Mountain-sea route: Qingdao → Weihai → Yantai, drive along the coast or take a train to enjoy the coastal scenery.
